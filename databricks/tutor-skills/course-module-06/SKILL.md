# course-module-06

Module 6 of AI Cyber Bootcamp 2026: Anomaly Detection.

## What this module teaches

Anomalies in a CPU-utilisation time series: seasonal decomposition, forecasting with ARIMA through pmdarima's auto-fit and with Prophet, and flagging a point by the size of its residual against the forecast. Why the base rate makes anomaly detection an alerting problem rather than a modelling one, and how a threshold trades recall for analyst time.
